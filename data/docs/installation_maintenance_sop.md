# Installation & Maintenance — Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

## Scheduling an installation
1. After an order is delivered, request an installation slot through support.
2. A site-readiness checklist is shared (power, flooring, shielding, HVAC).
3. Once the site passes readiness, a certified engineer is scheduled — typically within 5–10 business days.
4. Installation includes assembly, calibration, acceptance testing, and staff hand-over training.

## Booking preventive maintenance
- Preventive maintenance (PM) frequency depends on your AMC plan (Basic: 1/yr, Standard: 2/yr, Premium: 4/yr).
- Request a PM visit through support; you will receive a confirmed date and technician name.
- PM covers inspection, cleaning, calibration verification, and safety checks.

## Requesting a technician visit
For an unplanned issue, register a complaint ticket describing the fault. The response time follows your AMC plan (24/48/72 hours). Critical faults (patient-safety impact) are escalated immediately regardless of plan.

## Site requirements (typical)
- Dedicated 3-phase power with stable earth.
- Ambient temperature 18–22 °C, humidity 40–60%.
- For MRI: RF-shielded room (Faraday cage) and cryogen vent.
- For CT: lead shielding per local radiation-safety code.

## After-service
Every service visit closes with a service report. If an issue is unresolved, the ticket can be escalated to L2 support.
