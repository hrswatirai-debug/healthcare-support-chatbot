# MagnaScan 1.5T MRI — User Manual (Excerpt)

## Product specifications
- Model: MagnaScan 1.5T MRI (EQ-MRI-15T)
- Field strength: 1.5 Tesla
- Bore diameter: 70 cm
- Gradient strength: 45 mT/m
- Software: MagnaOS v8.2 (compatible with MagnaOS v7.5 and above)
- Power: 380–415 V, 3-phase, 50/60 Hz
- Operating temperature: 18–22 °C; humidity 40–60% non-condensing
- Weight: 4,200 kg (magnet assembly)

## Software compatibility
MagnaOS v8.2 is backward compatible with imaging protocols created in v7.5+. DICOM 3.0 export is supported to any PACS system. Third-party post-processing suites that support DICOM-RT are compatible.

## Daily start-up procedure
1. Confirm the chiller and cooling pump are running (green status LED).
2. Power on the operator console; wait for MagnaOS to finish self-test.
3. Run the daily QA phantom scan before the first patient.
4. Verify helium level is above 60% on the cryogen gauge.

## Cleaning and care
Wipe the patient table and bore with a non-abrasive, non-alcoholic disinfectant approved for MRI environments. Never bring ferromagnetic cleaning tools into the scan room. Clean the RF coils with a lightly dampened lint-free cloth only.

## Safety
Only MRI-safe equipment may enter Zone IV (the magnet room). Screen every patient for implants before entry. The magnet is ALWAYS on, even when the console is powered off.

## Common warnings
- "Cryogen low": schedule a helium refill; do not scan below 40%.
- "Gradient over-temperature": allow a 15-minute cool-down; check chiller flow.
- For persistent faults, register a complaint ticket so a field-service technician is dispatched.
