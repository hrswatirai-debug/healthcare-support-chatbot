# ClariCT 64-Slice Scanner — User Manual (Excerpt)

## Product specifications
- Model: ClariCT 64-Slice Scanner (EQ-CT-64)
- Slices per rotation: 64
- Rotation time: 0.35 s
- Gantry aperture: 78 cm
- Tube voltage: 80–140 kV
- Software: ClariOS v5.1 (DICOM 3.0 compliant)
- Power: 380–415 V, 3-phase

## Compatibility
ClariOS v5.1 exports DICOM 3.0 to any standard PACS. Dose-tracking integrates with DICOM Radiation Dose Structured Reports (RDSR). Compatible contrast injectors: any device supporting the CAN-bus sync interface.

## Start-up and warm-up
1. Power the gantry and console; wait for ClariOS boot.
2. Run tube warm-up (required if idle > 4 hours).
3. Perform daily air calibration before clinical scanning.

## Calibration warning at startup
A "Calibration warning" on startup usually means the daily air calibration was skipped or ambient temperature drifted. Re-run air calibration from Service > Calibration. If the warning persists after re-calibration, log a complaint ticket for a technician visit.

## Cleaning
Wipe the gantry aperture and patient table with an approved medical disinfectant. Keep liquids away from the tube housing vents.

## Maintenance
Preventive maintenance is recommended every 6 months and includes tube inspection, cooling check, and calibration verification. Book preventive maintenance through support.
