# Warranty & Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) Policy

## Standard warranty
All new equipment ships with a **24-month standard warranty** from the date of installation. The warranty covers manufacturing defects, parts, and labor for covered failures. It does not cover consumables, physical/accidental damage, or damage from unauthorized modification.

## AMC plans
After (or during) the warranty period, customers may enroll in an Annual Maintenance Contract:

| Plan | Preventive visits/year | Response time | Parts covered | Emergency support |
|------|------------------------|---------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Basic | 1 | 72 hours | No | Business hours |
| Standard | 2 | 48 hours | Wear parts | Business hours |
| Premium | 4 | 24 hours | All parts | 24/7 |

## Enrollment and upgrades
- You can enroll in an AMC at any time; pricing depends on the equipment category.
- Upgrades (e.g., Standard → Premium) take effect from the next billing cycle.
- To extend a warranty or upgrade an AMC, contact support; a technician confirms eligibility.

## Coverage status
"Active" means the contract is currently valid. "Expired" means it has lapsed and must be renewed to restore coverage. "NotEnrolled" means no AMC is currently in place, so only remaining standard warranty (if any) applies.

## Claims
To claim warranty/AMC service, register a complaint ticket. Covered claims are dispatched to a field-service team according to your plan's response time.
