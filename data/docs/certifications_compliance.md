# Certifications & Compliance

## Available certificates
All MediCorp equipment is supplied with the following certificates on request:
- **ISO 13485:2016** — Medical devices quality management system.
- **CE Marking** — Conformity with EU Medical Device Regulation (MDR 2017/745).
- **US FDA 510(k) clearance** — for applicable imaging devices.
- **IEC 60601-1** — Medical electrical equipment safety compliance.

To request a copy of any certificate for your equipment, ask support and the PDF will be shared via the customer portal.

## Regulatory compliance
MediCorp equipment is manufactured under an ISO 13485 quality system. Devices meet IEC 60601 electrical-safety and electromagnetic-compatibility standards. Radiation-emitting devices (CT, X-ray) comply with applicable dose-management regulations (e.g., NEMA XR-29 / DICOM RDSR dose reporting).

## Healthcare data handling
Customer support interactions are handled under a HIPAA-like posture: identity is verified before any account data is shared, data access is limited to the authenticated client, and all interactions are logged for audit. Support agents and the chatbot never disclose one client's data to another.

## Documentation policy
Manuals, certificates, and Standard Operating Procedures are controlled documents. The latest approved version is always the one distributed through the official support portal.
