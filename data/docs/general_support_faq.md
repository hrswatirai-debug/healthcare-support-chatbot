# General Support FAQ

## Business hours
Customer support is available Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM (local time). Premium AMC customers have 24/7 emergency support.

## How to contact support
- Chatbot (this assistant) for instant answers.
- Email: support@medicorp.example
- Phone: +1-800-555-0199 (emergency line for Premium customers).

## Documentation policy
Manuals, certificates, and SOPs are controlled documents distributed only through the official portal. Always use the latest approved version.

## SonoPro Ultrasound — quick specs
- Model: SonoPro Ultrasound (EQ-US-PRO)
- Probes: linear, curved, phased-array (L12-5 supported)
- Software: SonoOS v3.4, DICOM export supported
- Portable cart-based system

## VitalGuard Patient Monitor — quick specs
- Model: VitalGuard Patient Monitor (EQ-MON-VS)
- Parameters: ECG, SpO2, NIBP, temperature, respiration
- Display: 15-inch touchscreen
- Connectivity: HL7 / central-station compatible

## Refunds and credit notes
Refund and credit-note requests are reviewed within 7 business days. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method; a credit note is issued when a refund cannot be processed directly.

## What the chatbot can help with
Order & delivery status, product specs and manuals, installation and maintenance scheduling, warranty and AMC queries, complaint registration and tracking, payment and invoice queries, spare-part availability, certificates and compliance, and general questions. If it cannot find an answer, it will say so honestly.
